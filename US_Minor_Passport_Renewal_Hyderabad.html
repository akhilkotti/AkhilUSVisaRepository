U.S. Child Passport Renewal / Reissuance

Step-by-step guide for a U.S.-born child under 16 who is currently in Hyderabad, Telangana, with both parents available to attend the U.S. Consulate appointment.

Child under 16Born in the United StatesBoth parents in HyderabadForm DS-11
Key point: A child under 16 cannot use the adult passport renewal process. A new in-person child passport application is required each time, using Form DS-11. The child and both parents must appear together. The child's passport is normally valid for five years.

The parents' previous H-1B employment and their return to India do not cancel or change the child's U.S. citizenship. The child's U.S. birth certificate is primary evidence of citizenship and also usually establishes the parent-child relationship when it lists both parents.

2. Complete document checklist

Child's documents

  • Completed Form DS-11, printed single-sided; do not sign until instructed by the consular officer.
  • Original current or most recently issued U.S. passport, even if expired.
  • Original certified U.S. birth certificate showing the child's full name, date/place of birth and parent(s).
  • Photocopy of the U.S. birth certificate and/or other citizenship evidence as directed.
  • One recent U.S.-compliant passport photograph.
  • Appointment confirmation and any application/payment confirmation requested by the Consulate.

Both parents' documents

  • Both parents must attend in person with the child.
  • Original government-issued photo ID for each parent (for example, valid passport); bring photocopies.
  • Bring parents' passports and any additional photo identification available.
  • If a parent's name differs from the name on the birth certificate, bring supporting legal name-change evidence, such as a marriage certificate or court order.

Practical extras

  • Photocopies of all supporting documents, kept separately from originals.
  • Any previous U.S. passport or citizenship document in the child's possession.
  • For a lost or stolen valid passport, follow the additional reporting requirements and bring the relevant loss/theft statement or report as instructed.
  • For a damaged passport, bring the damaged passport and a written explanation; the Consulate may require additional documentation.
DS-3053 is not normally needed in this case because both parents will attend in person with the child. DS-3053 is generally used when one parent cannot appear and the other parent is applying with the child.

3. Step-by-step application procedure

  1. Review the official child passport instructions.
    Read the U.S. Department of State's under-16 guidance and the Hyderabad Consulate's American Citizen Services instructions.
  2. Complete Form DS-11.
    Use the online form filler or download the PDF. Enter the child's details exactly as shown on the U.S. birth certificate and previous passport. Print single-sided. Do not sign.
  3. Prepare citizenship and relationship evidence.
    Take the original certified U.S. birth certificate and the child's previous U.S. passport, plus copies. Bring both parents' original photo IDs and copies.
  4. Get the child's passport photo.
    Use a recent 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm) colour photo with a plain white/off-white background, meeting the official U.S. photo requirements. Do not staple it to the form.
  5. Check the Hyderabad appointment process.
    Use the official U.S. Mission India / Hyderabad American Citizen Services instructions to confirm the current appointment method, submission requirements and payment method. Do not assume a U.S. domestic passport acceptance-facility process applies abroad.
  6. Attend in person.
    Child and both parents attend the appointment at the U.S. Consulate General in Hyderabad. Bring all originals, photocopies, photo, printed DS-11 and appointment confirmation. Sign DS-11 only when the consular officer instructs you.
  7. Pay the applicable overseas consular fee.
    Follow the Consulate's current instructions for online payment or accepted payment methods. The fee table below distinguishes India overseas fees from U.S. domestic fees.
  8. Follow the Consulate's collection/delivery instructions.
    Keep the receipt and application reference. Follow the instructions given at the appointment for passport collection or delivery and return of original supporting documents.

5. Complete child passport instructions (under 16)

A. In-person application is mandatory

  • Every child under 16 must apply in person for each passport.
  • The child must appear with both parents or legal guardians, unless an accepted alternative consent/authority document is provided.
  • A child passport is generally valid for five years and cannot be renewed using Form DS-82.

B. Complete and print the application

  • Use Form DS-11. Complete it online or by hand in black ink, following the form instructions.
  • Print single-sided; do not submit a double-sided application.
  • Do not sign the application before the appointment. The child/parent signs when directed by the consular officer.
  • Enter names, dates and places of birth consistently with the birth certificate and previous passport. Explain any legal name changes with supporting documents.

C. Prove U.S. citizenship

Acceptable citizenship evidence can include a U.S. birth certificate, a Consular Report of Birth Abroad, a Certificate of Citizenship, or a qualifying full-validity U.S. passport. For this U.S.-born child, bring the original certified U.S. birth certificate and the previous U.S. passport. A phone image or digital copy is not a substitute for required physical evidence.

D. Prove the parent-child relationship

The U.S. birth certificate normally provides the child's details and names of the parents. If it does not establish the relationship, bring the relevant adoption decree, custody order or other legal document. If a parent's current legal name differs from the name shown on the birth certificate, bring evidence connecting the names.

E. Parental consent and attendance

Both parents should appear with the child and present original government-issued photo IDs. If only one parent can attend, the absent parent's notarized DS-3053 and ID copy may be required. Consent documents generally must be submitted within 90 days of signing/notarization. Follow the overseas post's specific instructions.

F. Passport photograph

  • One colour photo, 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm).
  • Taken within the last six months, showing the child's current appearance.
  • Plain white or off-white background; face directly toward the camera with a neutral expression or natural smile and eyes open.
  • No glasses. Head coverings are allowed only for qualifying religious or medical reasons, with required statements.
  • Do not digitally alter the image or use filters. Do not staple or attach it to DS-11; bring it loose.

G. Special situations

  • Lost or stolen passport: report it as required and follow the Consulate's additional DS-64 / replacement instructions.
  • Damaged passport: submit the damaged passport and a signed explanation; additional evidence may be requested.
  • Urgent travel: contact Hyderabad ACS before the appointment and explain the travel date. Do not assume U.S.-domestic expedited service or domestic delivery options apply to an overseas application.
  • Previous passport: take it even if expired. The Consulate will advise whether it must be retained or returned during processing.

6. U.S. passport fees – separate fee guide

Important: overseas fees are different from U.S. domestic fees. The $100 + $35 figures often shown on the State Department's domestic child passport page are for applications at U.S. domestic acceptance facilities. They should not be used as the Hyderabad consular fee quote.

A. Published U.S. Mission India overseas fee schedule

The U.S. Mission India American Citizen Services fee page lists the following fees for minor applicants. Its displayed rupee conversion is based on a consular exchange rate of ₹92 per US$1, effective September 29, 2025. Exchange rates and payment instructions can change; verify the live amount before paying.

Minor passport book and card$150₹13,800

Likely selection for international travel: A passport book. A passport card is not valid for international air travel; it is limited to qualifying land and sea travel to Canada, Mexico, Bermuda and the Caribbean.

B. Payment methods in India

The U.S. Mission India ACS fee page says minor applicants applying in person are recommended to use the U.S. government's secure Pay.gov payment site. It also lists cash U.S. dollars, cash Indian rupees at the current consular exchange rate, demand draft at the current rate, or credit card as alternatives. Personal checks are not accepted. Confirm the current payment workflow and exact amount with Hyderabad ACS before making payment; online payment availability and instructions can change.

C. Optional / additional fees

  • Expedited service: The standard $60 U.S.-domestic expedite fee is not applicable to passport applications submitted at U.S. embassies/consulates abroad. Ask Hyderabad ACS about urgent travel handling.
  • Passport card: It is optional and generally not useful as a substitute for a passport book for international air travel.
  • File search: A separate fee may apply if citizenship/previous passport records must be searched because acceptable evidence is unavailable. Avoid this by bringing the original U.S. birth certificate and previous passport, if available.
Fee verification: The India ACS fee page currently surfaced in this research displays the ₹92 exchange rate effective September 29, 2025. Treat the INR figures as a reference only, not a guaranteed November 2026 payment amount. Confirm the live fee and payment instructions on the official India ACS fee page or by contacting Hyderabad ACS.

  1. Open the official Hyderabad Consulate website and locate American Citizen Services / U.S. passports.
  2. Follow the current passport appointment instructions for U.S. citizens in India. Use the appointment system or contact method linked by the official site.
  3. Do not book a U.S. visa appointment. This is a U.S. citizen passport service, not a visa application.
  4. If the website does not clearly show the appointment route, email hydacs@state.gov with the child's age, U.S. place of birth, current location, passport status and the fact that both parents can attend.
  5. Do not send sensitive identity documents by ordinary email unless the Consulate specifically instructs you to do so.

Hyderabad provides U.S. citizen services for Telangana, Andhra Pradesh and Odisha. Confirm the current jurisdiction and appointment instructions on the official Consulate page.

8. After submitting the application

  • Keep the application receipt and any case/reference number given by the Consulate.
  • Follow the Consulate's instructions for tracking, collection or courier delivery. Overseas processing and delivery times can differ from U.S. domestic processing estimates.
  • Check that the child's name, date of birth, place of birth and other printed details are correct when the new passport is received.
  • Keep the new passport and returned birth certificate / supporting documents safely.
  • Plan travel only after confirming the new passport is issued and available. If travel is imminent, tell ACS before the appointment.
