4. Interview Tips

  • Keep answers short, direct and truthful.
  • Answer the question asked; do not give a long explanation unless requested.
  • Maintain natural eye contact and speak calmly.
  • Do not memorize a speech word-for-word. Understand the facts.
  • Do not hide the previous refusal.
  • Do not say you will work, seek employment, or settle in the US if that is not your actual purpose.
  • Make sure answers about dates, university, course, address, finances and travel plans are accurate.
